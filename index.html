WeatherGPT

Multilingual AI-Powered IVR Telephony Platform for Agro-Meteorological Advisory & Disaster Broadcast

WeatherGPT is an AI-powered multilingual Interactive Voice Response (IVR) telephony platform engineered to bridge critical information gaps for rural agrarian communities. It delivers precise, automated agro-meteorological advisory and emergency disaster broadcasts based on real climate telemetry fetched directly from official Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) sources, combined with domain-verified agro advisories from the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) and State Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs).

▹ Official IMD Data Ingestion: Real-time climate telemetry parsing for early alerts.
▹ ICAR & State KVKs Advisory: Scientifically backed crop & livestock recommendations.
▹ Multilingual IVR Telephony: Inclusive voice interface accessible even on basic feature phones.
